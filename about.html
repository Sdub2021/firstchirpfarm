Story and principles

About the farm

First Chirp Farm began as a simple question: what if a piece of land could hold a sanctuary and a school at the same time — without one using the other up?

The name is the morning itself. Before tools, before plans, there is a first chirp. Birds. Rescued hens finding their voices. Children arriving and going quiet because something living is looking back at them.

This page is a working draft of the story. Details of place, people, and dates will be filled in as the farm is ready to share them. The ethics do not wait for a perfect origin myth.

Three ethics

Earth Care

Build soil, hold water, plant diversity, leave edges wild. The farm is designed so fertility rises instead of leaving in a truck.

People Care

Children, volunteers, neighbours, and anyone who arrives tired. Learning is slow, supervised, and honest about risk and kindness.

Fair Share

Surplus food, knowledge, and time move outward. We do not take more animals than we can love well. Capacity is a form of ethics.

How we work

  • Observe first. The land and the animals set the pace. Plans follow patterns, not the other way around.
  • Stack functions. A hedge is food, shelter, windbreak, and a classroom wall.
  • Small and slow. Better one well-kept paddock than ten stressed ones.
  • Transparency. If we cannot take another rescue this month, we say so.
