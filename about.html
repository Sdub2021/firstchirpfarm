About the farm

First Chirp Farm began as a simple question: what if a piece of land could hold a sanctuary and a school at the same time without one using the other up?

The name is the morning itself. Before tools, before plans, there is a first chirp.

This page is a working draft of the story. Details of place and dates can be filled in next.

Earth Care

Build soil, hold water, plant diversity, leave edges wild.

People Care

Children, volunteers, neighbours. Learning is slow, supervised, and kind.

Fair Share

We do not take more animals than we can love well. Capacity is ethics.
