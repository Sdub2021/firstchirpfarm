What this place is

Land that heals. Animals that stay. Children who learn.

First Chirp Farm is being built as a living system: food growing in guilds, animals living as partners rather than products, and a school where children meet the work of care face to face.

Named for the first sounds of the day — birds, rescued hens, the small voices that arrive with the morning — the farm is a place to practice Earth Care, People Care, and Fair Share.
