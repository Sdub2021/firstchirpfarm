Children’s programmes

The school

The farm is the curriculum. Children learn by doing work that matters: watering, watching, waiting, asking better questions. Dates and fees will be published when sessions are confirmed.

Morning sessions

Small groups. Seed to harvest, compost, quiet time with the flock.

Seasonal days

Planting, harvest, leaf-fall, first frost.

Family visits

Guided walks. Slow, supervised, no unscheduled animal handling.

Safeguarding

Children are never left alone with animals or tools. Formal policy to be added here.
